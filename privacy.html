Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Overfly shows you the aircraft, satellites, Moon and planets above you. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Location

Overfly uses your device’s location to find aircraft near you and to work out the direction and distance to each one.

  • Your precise location stays on your device. It’s used only to calculate distances and directions.
  • An approximate location is sent to flight-data providers. To ask which aircraft are nearby, Overfly sends a position rounded to about 1 km to the live flight-data services listed below. No account, name, device identifier or other personal information is sent with it.
  • Background location is used only while you follow a flight. When you choose Follow on Lock Screen, Overfly keeps using your location in the background so the flight’s distance on your Lock Screen stays accurate. iOS shows a blue location indicator while this is on. It stops when you stop following, or when the flight lands.

Overfly doesn’t store your location history, and doesn’t sell or share your location for advertising or tracking.

Other data

  • No accounts, analytics or advertising. Overfly has no sign-in and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Pass reminders. If you ask to be reminded about a satellite pass, Overfly schedules a notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Settings stay on your device. Your choices, such as units and radar range, are stored only on your device.
  • Aircraft photos. When you open a flight, Overfly asks Planespotters.net for a photo using the aircraft’s public identifiers (its registration or transponder code), not your data.

Third-party services

Overfly requests live data from these services. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • adsb.fi and adsb.lol: live aircraft positions (receive your approximate location)
  • Planespotters.net: aircraft photos (receives only the aircraft’s registration or transponder code)
  • CelesTrak: satellite orbit data, downloaded at most twice a day (receives no location or personal data)

Routes, airports, airlines and aircraft details are built into the app from public data (VRS Standing Data and the US FAA aircraft registry), so looking them up sends nothing off your device. Satellite passes, the Moon and planets are calculated on your device.

Children

Overfly isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from them.
